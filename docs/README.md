# OctoAcme Project Management

This directory is the working knowledge base for OctoAcme project management. It describes how teams initiate, plan, deliver, communicate, release, and improve projects, with practical guidance, checklists, templates, and role definitions.

## Process Overview

OctoAcme uses a customer-first, iterative process with clear ownership, data-informed decisions, and psychological safety. Work moves through five connected stages:

1. **Initiation:** validate the business need, define measurable outcomes, identify stakeholders, establish initial risks and resources, and make a go/no-go decision for planning.
2. **Planning:** turn approved work into a prioritized backlog with acceptance criteria, estimates, milestones, dependencies, a Definition of Done, and an initial quality approach.
3. **Execution and tracking:** deliver in small increments using a project board, focused pull requests, automated quality checks, regular standups, delivery syncs, and demos. Teams track progress, metrics, blockers, and escalations.
4. **Risk, communication, release, and deployment:** maintain a risk register, use agreed communication cadences, meet pre-release quality and rollback requirements, verify staging and production deployments, and announce releases to stakeholders.
5. **Retrospective and continuous improvement:** after sprints, releases, milestones, or incidents, capture what went well, identify improvements, assign action items, and review their impact.

Project managers, product managers, and developers share responsibility for turning decisions into visible work and keeping stakeholders informed. The process documents define the artifacts, handoffs, quality gates, escalation paths, and communication practices that support that collaboration.
